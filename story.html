Our Story
Berrydall Productions began with our first short feature film, The Gorilla Chase, in 2024. Before this, we had put together a few videos as recaps of things like our circus, but nothing like The Gorilla Chase. In February of 2022, the Berrymans and Sullens families teamed up to film The Gorilla Chase, and it was a great success. The Berrymans soon realized they enjoyed filmaking a lot, and two years later they came out with The Front Entry Conspiracy, themed after the film Defense of New Haven by Little Crew Studios. The Front Entry Conspiracy features the City Defense Force: Lou, Alec, and Elsie, and the mayor and his helper. The mayor has become corrupt and it is only up to the CDF to help. Then the orphan named Two, and the secret Agent Jill show up. It is up to the CDF who to trust, and their lives may depend on it.
